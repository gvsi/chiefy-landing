Most people have more than one inbox — a work Gmail, a personal Outlook, a shared booking address. Connected Mailboxes brings them together so Chiefy can read across all of them from a single login. You can connect up to eight mailboxes, in any mix of Gmail and Outlook.

## Before you start

You'll need to be signed in to Chiefy with the mailbox you already use. That first mailbox — the one you log in with — is your sign-in mailbox, and it's always connected. Everything below is about adding another one alongside it.

> New to Chiefy and connecting your first inbox? Start with [Connect Gmail or Outlook](/help/getting-started/connect-gmail-or-outlook) instead.

## What happens after you connect

A newly connected mailbox is on by default. As soon as it finishes syncing, its email is searchable and available to [Ask Chiefy](/help/ask-chiefy), so you can ask questions across all your inboxes at once.

If you'd rather keep a mailbox synced but leave it out of search and assistant answers, each row has a **Use with Chiefy** toggle you can turn off. Your sign-in mailbox stays on — it can't be turned off, because you use it to log in.

> Reading across mailboxes and writing on them are separate things. For exactly what runs automatically on a secondary mailbox, see [What automates on secondary mailboxes](/help/connected-mailboxes/what-automates-on-secondary-mailboxes).

## Set up Smart Drafts per mailbox

Smart Drafts can be tuned for each mailbox on its own. For every connected mailbox you can:

- Turn Smart Drafts on or off for that mailbox
- Set how often it offers a draft (its sensitivity)
- Choose the signature it uses

So your work mailbox and your personal mailbox can behave completely differently. To turn on the automatic mode, see [Turn on Autodraft](/help/smart-drafts/turn-on-autodraft).

## Limits and common errors

A few things to know going in:

- **Eight mailboxes, total.** You can connect up to eight non-disconnected mailboxes across Gmail and Outlook combined, including your sign-in mailbox. If you reach the limit, disconnect one you no longer need before adding another.
- **The sign-in mailbox can't be disconnected.** It's the identity you log in with, so it always stays connected.
- **A mailbox can only belong to one Chiefy account.** If the address is already connected to a different Chiefy account, you'll see an error. Disconnect it from that account first, or contact support to move it over.
- **Permission needed.** If the connection fails with a permission error, run it again and accept the Gmail or Outlook mail access when prompted.
