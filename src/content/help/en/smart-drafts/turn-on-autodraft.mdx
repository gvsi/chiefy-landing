<HelpTldr>
  Autodraft is the automatic mode of Smart Drafts. Turn it on and Chiefy quietly prepares a reply for the emails that need one, so a draft is already waiting when you open the thread. Nothing is ever sent for you.
</HelpTldr>

Smart Drafts can work two ways. **On demand**, you ask Chiefy for a reply whenever you want one. With **Autodraft** on, Chiefy does that step ahead of you — it watches for emails that look like they need a response and writes a draft in your voice, ready to review. You stay in control: Autodraft only ever creates drafts, it never sends.

## Before you start

You'll need the Chiefy extension installed and your inbox connected. New to Chiefy? Start with [Getting started](/help/getting-started) first.

> Autodraft runs on your primary mailbox by default. To control it for each account, see [Connected Mailboxes](/help/connected-mailboxes).

## Turn on Autodraft

<HelpSteps>
  <HelpStep title="Open Chiefy settings">
    In Gmail, open the Chiefy side panel and choose **Settings**.
  </HelpStep>
  <HelpStep title="Go to Smart Drafts">
    Select **Smart Drafts** from the settings menu.
  </HelpStep>
  <HelpStep title="Switch Autodraft on" anno="A">
    Toggle **Autodraft** on. Chiefy starts preparing drafts for new replies right away — no need to refresh.

## What you'll see

When Autodraft has prepared a reply, it's waiting as a draft on the thread — the same draft marker Gmail shows for any saved draft. Open the thread and your reply is already in the box — read it, adjust anything, and send when you're happy.

## What Autodraft won't touch

Autodraft is deliberately conservative — it would rather skip a thread than put words in your mouth. It leaves alone:

- Newsletters, receipts, and notifications that don't need a reply
- Sensitive topics — legal, financial, HR — where it holds back on purpose
- Threads where it doesn't have enough context to be genuinely useful

> **Autodraft never sends.** Every reply waits for you to review and send it yourself.

## Turn Autodraft off

Open **Chiefy → Settings → Smart Drafts** and toggle **Autodraft** off. On-demand Smart Drafts keeps working — you can still ask Chiefy for a reply any time.
