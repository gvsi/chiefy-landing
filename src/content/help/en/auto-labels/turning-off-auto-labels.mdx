<HelpTldr>
  Auto Labels has one master toggle that controls all automatic labeling. Turn it off and Chiefy stops adding system labels to new emails. You can also leave it on and adjust each category's "Keep in inbox" setting to control which emails get archived.
</HelpTldr>

Auto Labels sorts your incoming email into the right category automatically — Action needed, Awaiting reply, and the rest. If it's doing too much, you have two levers: a single master toggle that turns the whole thing off, and a per-category "Keep in inbox" setting that controls archiving. This guide covers both.

## Turn off Auto Labels entirely

The master toggle is the global control. When you switch it off, Chiefy stops applying every system category label to new emails.

<HelpSteps>
  <HelpStep title="Open Chiefy settings">
    In Gmail, open the Chiefy side panel and choose **Settings**.
  </HelpStep>
  <HelpStep title="Go to Auto Labels">
    Select **Auto Labels** from the settings menu.
  </HelpStep>
  <HelpStep title="Switch the master toggle off" anno="A">
    Toggle **Auto Labels** off. Chiefy stops labeling new emails right away.

> While the master toggle is off, Chiefy skips system labeling completely — no labels are added or removed on new emails. Turning it back on resumes labeling from the next email onward.

## What stays and what stops

Turning off the master toggle changes future behavior only. It's worth knowing exactly what it does and doesn't touch.

- **New emails stop getting system labels.** With the toggle off, Chiefy won't apply Action needed, Awaiting reply, or any other system category label to incoming threads.
- **Existing labels stay put.** Threads that were already labeled keep their labels. Turning off the master toggle doesn't go back and strip labels from past emails.
- **Your own categories keep working.** Categories you've created yourself run independently of the master toggle. They continue to label and, where you've set them to, archive — even with Auto Labels' system categories switched off.

## Control archiving per category

If you mostly like Auto Labels but want to stop certain categories from disappearing into the archive, you don't need the master toggle at all. Each system category has its own **Keep in inbox** setting.

This setting controls archiving only — it's separate from whether the label gets applied. When **Keep in inbox** is on, a matching email is labeled and stays in your inbox. When it's off, the email is labeled and then archived, so the only place you'll find it is under its label.

To change it, open **Chiefy → Settings → Auto Labels**, find the category, and switch its **Keep in inbox** setting on or off.

> Changing **Keep in inbox** only affects future emails. It doesn't reach back and re-file emails Chiefy has already sorted — an email that was archived under a category stays archived, and one already in your inbox stays there.

## Which approach should you use

- **Stop all automatic labeling** — turn the master toggle off. Best when you want a quiet inbox with no system categories at all.
- **Keep labeling, stop a category from archiving** — leave the master toggle on and switch that category's **Keep in inbox** setting on. Best when the sorting is helpful but you don't want certain emails leaving your inbox.
