Getting started with Chiefy takes a few minutes. You connect your inbox, move through a brief onboarding flow, and land on a confirmation screen with a clear next step. This guide walks you through what to expect at each step and where you end up.

## Before you start

You'll need to connect a mailbox first. If you haven't yet, see [Connect Gmail or Outlook](/help/getting-started/connect-gmail-or-outlook). To use Chiefy directly inside Gmail, you'll also want the extension — see [Install the Chiefy extension](/help/getting-started/install-the-chiefy-extension).

> Chiefy's features stay locked until you have an active plan. Starting the free trial during onboarding unlocks everything.

## Step through onboarding

After you sign in, Chiefy guides you through three short screens.

<HelpSteps>
  <HelpStep title="Feature preview">
    The first screen is a short, animated preview while Chiefy gets ready. You'll see messages about analyzing your communication patterns, setting up, and optimizing your workflows. This screen is purely visual — nothing is being read or changed in your inbox yet.

    When it finishes, choose **Transform Your Inbox** to continue.
  </HelpStep>
  <HelpStep title="Choose your plan">
    Next you'll see the Pro plan with a 14-day free trial. Pick monthly or annual, then start your trial through secure checkout. You can cancel anytime.

## Where you land

The confirmation screen offers a clear next step:

- After you start your trial, you'll see **Go to Dashboard**, which opens your Chiefy dashboard. If you have the extension installed, Chiefy is already running in Gmail — just switch back to that tab whenever you're ready.

> If you already have a plan and you're an extension user, Chiefy skips the plan screen entirely and shows a **Go to Gmail** button instead — it takes you straight back to your inbox, no setup needed.

## What you'll see in the side panel

Open Chiefy from the side panel in Gmail and the tabs you see depend on your plan.

- On Pro, Chiefy opens straight into **Ask Chiefy** — the assistant you can chat with about your email and calendar — and there's no Home tab.
- If you haven't started a plan yet, or your plan has lapsed, you'll also get a **Home** tab with a quick way to start the trial, upgrade to Pro, or reactivate your plan.

## Start using Chiefy

With your trial active, every feature is unlocked. A few good first moves:

- Open a long thread and ask for a quick brief with [Smart Summaries](/help/smart-summaries/summarize-a-thread).
- Ask Chiefy to find an email, check your calendar, or draft a reply.
- Let Chiefy sort your inbox so what needs action stands out from the noise.

> Want replies waiting for you automatically? See [Turn on Autodraft](/help/smart-drafts/turn-on-autodraft).
