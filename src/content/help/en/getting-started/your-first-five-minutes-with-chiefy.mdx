Getting started with Chiefy takes a few minutes. You connect your inbox, move through a brief onboarding flow, and land on a confirmation screen that sends you straight to Gmail. This guide walks you through what to expect at each step and where you end up.

## Before you start

You'll need to connect a mailbox first. If you haven't yet, see [Connect Gmail or Outlook](/help/getting-started/connect-gmail-or-outlook). To use Chiefy directly inside Gmail, you'll also want the extension — see [Install the Chiefy extension](/help/getting-started/install-the-chiefy-extension).

> Chiefy's features stay locked until you have an active plan. Starting the free trial during onboarding unlocks everything.

## Where you land

The confirmation screen offers a clear next step, and it depends on how you signed up:

- If you came in through the **Chiefy extension**, you'll see **Go to Gmail** — it takes you back to your inbox with Chiefy already running.
- If you signed up on the **web**, you'll see **Go to Dashboard**, which opens your Chiefy dashboard.

> If you already finished onboarding earlier and you're an extension user, Chiefy skips the plan screen and shows a **Go to Gmail** button instead — no need to set up again.

## What you'll see in the side panel

Open Chiefy from the side panel in Gmail and you'll see a couple of tabs.

- While you're still on a trial-eligible or expired plan, a **Home** tab appears with an option to upgrade to Pro or reactivate your plan.
- Once you're on Pro, Chiefy goes straight to **Ask Chiefy** — the assistant you can chat with about your email and calendar.

## Start using Chiefy

With your trial active, every feature is unlocked. A few good first moves:

- Open a long thread and ask for a quick brief with [Smart Summaries](/help/smart-summaries/summarize-a-thread).
- Ask Chiefy to find an email, check your calendar, or draft a reply.
- Let Chiefy sort your inbox so what needs action stands out from the noise.

> Want replies waiting for you automatically? See [Turn on Autodraft](/help/smart-drafts/turn-on-autodraft).
