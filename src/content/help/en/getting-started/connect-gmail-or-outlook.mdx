<HelpTldr>
  Sign in with Google for Gmail or with Microsoft for Outlook. Chiefy uses your provider's own sign-in screen — you authorize access there, and your password never touches Chiefy. Once you're in, your inbox is connected and a 14-day trial starts.

Connecting takes a moment. You sign in through Google or Microsoft, review what Chiefy is asking for, and approve it. From then on, Chiefy can read your inbox and prepare drafts in the side panel — all through your provider's secure sign-in, so you never share your password with us.

## Before you start

You'll need the Chiefy extension installed. New to Chiefy? Start with [Install the Chiefy extension](/help/getting-started/install-the-chiefy-extension) first, then come back here.

## Start sign-in

There are two ways to begin, and both lead to the same secure sign-in screen.

<HelpSteps>
  <HelpStep title="Open sign-in from Gmail or the web">
    Click the **Chiefy** icon in Gmail to open the side panel, or go to [app.chiefy.com](https://app.chiefy.com) directly. Either one starts the sign-in flow.

> Chiefy needs full mailbox access to do its job — reading threads to summarize them, and creating drafts in your voice. It only ever prepares drafts; nothing is sent without you.

## What access Chiefy asks for

Sign-in uses OAuth, the standard way to grant an app access without sharing your password. You authorize Chiefy on Google's or Microsoft's own screen, and you can revoke that access from your provider's account settings at any time.

- **Gmail** uses Google OAuth. Chiefy requests permission to read, compose, send, and manage your Gmail, plus your basic profile and email address to identify your account.
- **Outlook** uses Microsoft OAuth, which grants the equivalent access to your Outlook mailbox.

## What happens after you approve

Once you approve, Chiefy completes sign-in and your inbox is connected.

- You're signed in and a **14-day free trial** begins, with no charge until it ends. {/* VERIFY: the trial is created at Stripe checkout during the onboarding pricing step (trial_period_days: 14), and only if you haven't used a trial before — it does not start at the moment OAuth completes. Confirm the desired user-facing framing. */}
- You're taken into the onboarding flow — the pricing step, then a confirmation — so Chiefy is ready in your inbox.
- The extension notices you're signed in automatically. It watches a small sign-in marker that the connection sets, so it picks up sign-in, sign-out, and routine session refreshes without you doing anything. {/* VERIFY: extension monitors the non-HttpOnly duet_token_expiry companion cookie via browser.cookies.onChanged; the JWT access cookie itself is HttpOnly. Kept deliberately non-technical for users. */}

After that, reload Gmail if it's open and the Chiefy side panel is ready to use.

## If sign-in doesn't work

If something goes wrong, Chiefy shows a message tailored to your provider — Google or Microsoft — with guidance on what to try next. The most common cases:

- **Wrong account.** You signed in with a different account than the one you meant to connect. Sign out of that account with your provider, or start sign-in again and pick the right one.
- **Access not granted.** You closed the provider's screen or declined a permission Chiefy needs. Start sign-in again and approve the requested access — Chiefy can't read your inbox or prepare drafts without it.

If you approve everything and still can't connect, try signing in from [app.chiefy.com](https://app.chiefy.com) in a fresh tab, then reload Gmail.
