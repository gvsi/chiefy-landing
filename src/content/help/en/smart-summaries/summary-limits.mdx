  Smart Summaries comes with a monthly quota. Once you've used it up, asking for a new summary returns a "limit reached" message until your quota resets. Summaries you've already generated stay available, and your plan determines how many you get.
</HelpTldr>

Chiefy caps how many summaries you can generate each month. The cap is there to keep usage fair, and for most people it's far more than a typical month of email needs. When you reach it, Chiefy stops generating new summaries until the quota resets — but everything you've already summarized stays exactly where it is.

## How the limit works

Your summary quota is counted **per account, per month**. Every time Chiefy generates a new summary for you, that counts as one against your monthly total.

A few things to know:

- **Re-opening an existing summary doesn't count.** Asking for a summary of a thread Chiefy has already summarized just shows the saved version — it doesn't spend another summary.
- **The quota is tied to your plan.** Higher plans get a much larger monthly allowance. {/* RESOLVED 2026-06-16 (founder): use server-config numbers; the plans article states Free = 10 summaries/mo. Kept qualitative here to avoid number drift. */}
- **The count resets each month.** Once your window rolls over, your full allowance is available again.

## What happens when you hit the limit

When you've used your monthly allowance and ask for a new summary, Chiefy can't generate it. Instead of a summary you'll see a message telling you the limit has been reached, along with where your usage currently stands for the month.

This affects **new** summaries only. Any thread you've already summarized stays available to re-open at no cost.

> Reaching the limit won't break anything else in Chiefy. The rest of your features keep working normally — only new summary generation pauses until your quota resets.

## What to do when you hit the limit

If you've reached your monthly summary limit, you have a few options:

- **Wait for the reset.** Your quota refreshes at the start of your next monthly window, and new summaries work again automatically.
- **Re-use what you have.** Summaries you've already generated don't count against your quota, so you can keep opening those freely.
- **Upgrade your plan** for a much larger monthly allowance if you regularly run out. See [Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel](/help/account-billing/upgrade-downgrade-or-cancel).

## Automatic summaries and your quota

Some summaries happen in the background rather than from a button you tap — for example, while Chiefy is preparing a draft for a thread. These automatic summaries draw from the same monthly allowance.

The difference is in how a limit is handled. When you tap to summarize and you're out of quota, Chiefy tells you directly. When a background summary would go over the limit, Chiefy simply skips it quietly rather than interrupting you — so you won't see an error from work happening behind the scenes.

> If you notice a thread that didn't get an automatic summary near the end of your month, a reached quota is one likely reason. It will resume on its own once your quota resets.

To learn what summaries are and how they're generated, see [How summaries work](/help/smart-summaries/how-summaries-work). To generate one yourself, see [Summarize a thread](/help/smart-summaries/summarize-a-thread).
