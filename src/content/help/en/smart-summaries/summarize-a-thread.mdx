<HelpTldr>
  Open a long thread and Smart Summaries gives you a one-tap TL;DR — a short snippet, the key points as bullets, and, when it makes sense, a couple of suggested replies. It all appears right at the top of the thread, so you can catch up without scrolling.
</HelpTldr>

Smart Summaries reads a thread for you and hands back the gist: a brief snippet, a bullet-point rundown of what matters, and — when the thread looks like it needs a response — a few reply suggestions you can use as a starting point. The summary appears at the top of the thread in Gmail, so there's nothing extra to open.

## Before you start

You'll need the Chiefy extension installed and your inbox connected. New to Chiefy? Start with [Getting started](/help/getting-started) first.

> Smart Summaries works on the thread you're reading. To learn what goes into a summary, see [How summaries work](/help/smart-summaries/how-summaries-work).

## What the summary includes

A Smart Summary has up to three parts, all shown together at the top of the thread:

- **A snippet** — a one-line gist of where the thread stands.
- **Key points** — the thread's main beats as a short bullet list, so you can skim instead of reading every message.
- **Suggested replies** — when the thread looks like it needs a response, Chiefy offers a couple of reply starters alongside the summary. If a thread doesn't call for a reply, you simply won't see them.

## Where the summary appears

The summary shows up at the top of the thread in Gmail, inside the thread view itself — not as a separate popup or window. Once a thread has a summary, you'll see it the next time you open that thread, so there's nothing to re-run.

> If a thread already has a summary, the **Summarize this email** button won't appear — the summary is shown in its place.

## If you don't see the Summarize button

A few reasons the button might not show:

- **The thread is already summarized.** When a summary exists, Chiefy shows it instead of the button.
- **You've reached your summary limit.** Smart Summaries has a monthly limit. If you've hit it, Chiefy lets you know and points you to your plan. See [Summary limits](/help/smart-summaries/summary-limits) for the details.
- **The extension isn't connected.** Make sure Chiefy is installed and your inbox is connected — see [Getting started](/help/getting-started).
