<HelpTldr>
  Open a long thread and Smart Summaries gives you a one-tap TL;DR — the key points as bullets and, when it makes sense, a couple of suggested replies. It all appears right at the top of the thread, so you can catch up without scrolling.
</HelpTldr>

Smart Summaries reads a thread for you and hands back the gist: a bullet-point rundown of what matters and — when the thread looks like it needs a response — a few reply suggestions you can use as a starting point. The summary appears at the top of the thread in Gmail, so there's nothing extra to open.

## Before you start

You'll need the Chiefy extension installed and your inbox connected. New to Chiefy? Start with [Getting started](/help/getting-started) first.

> Smart Summaries works on the thread you're reading. To learn what goes into a summary, see [How summaries work](/help/smart-summaries/how-summaries-work).

## Summarize a thread

<HelpSteps>
  <HelpStep title="Open the thread">
    In Gmail, open the email thread you want to catch up on.
  </HelpStep>
  <HelpStep title="Select Summarize" anno="A">
    If Chiefy hasn't already summarized the thread, you'll see a **Summarize this email** button at the top of the thread. Select it, and Chiefy generates the summary.
    <SmartSummaryMock
      state="cta"
      figcaption="When no summary exists yet, Chiefy shows a Summarize this email button at the top of the thread."
    />
  </HelpStep>
  <HelpStep title="Read the summary">
    Chiefy replaces the button with the summary — the key points as bullets, and any reply suggestions below them. Everything stays at the top of the thread.
  </HelpStep>
</HelpSteps>

## What the summary includes

A Smart Summary has up to two parts, shown together at the top of the thread:

- **Key points** — the thread's main beats as a short bullet list (usually one to three bullets), so you can skim instead of reading every message.
- **Suggested replies** — when the thread looks like it needs a response, Chiefy offers a couple of reply starters alongside the summary. If a thread doesn't call for a reply — for example an automated notification — you simply won't see them.

## Where the summary appears

The summary shows up at the top of the thread in Gmail, inside the thread view itself — not as a separate popup or window. Once a thread has a summary, you'll see it the next time you open that thread, so there's nothing to re-run.

> If a thread already has a summary, the **Summarize this email** button won't appear — the summary is shown in its place.

## If you don't see the Summarize button

A couple of reasons the button might not appear:

- **The thread is already summarized.** When a summary exists, Chiefy shows it instead of the button.
- **The extension isn't connected.** Make sure Chiefy is installed and your inbox is connected — see [Getting started](/help/getting-started).

## If you've hit your summary limit

Smart Summaries has a monthly limit. When you've used it up, the **Summarize this email** button still appears, but selecting it won't generate a summary — instead Chiefy lets you know you've reached your limit and points you to your plan, where you can upgrade for more. See [Summary limits](/help/smart-summaries/summary-limits) for the details.
