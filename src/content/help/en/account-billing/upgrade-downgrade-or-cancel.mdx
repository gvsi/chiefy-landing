Your plan lives in one place: **Settings → Subscription & Billing**. From there you can move up to Pro, switch how often you're billed, or stop your subscription. Anything that touches your card — downgrades, cancellations, payment details — happens in the secure billing portal, which Chiefy opens for you.

## Find your plan settings

In the Chiefy web app, open **Settings**, then choose **Subscription & Billing**. This page shows your current plan, status, and next payment date.

The **Manage Payment** button — which opens the billing portal — appears only when you have an active, recurring Pro subscription. You won't see it if you're on Free, on Lifetime Pro, or if your subscription has already been canceled.

## Upgrade from Free to Pro

If you're on the Free plan, upgrading takes a moment:

<HelpSteps>
  <HelpStep title="Open the pricing page">
    Click **Upgrade** on the pricing page, or open **Settings → Subscription & Billing** — Free accounts are sent straight to pricing.
  </HelpStep>
  <HelpStep title="Choose your plan">
    Pick monthly or yearly billing and continue to secure checkout.
  </HelpStep>
  <HelpStep title="Start your free trial">
    If you haven't used a trial before, your Pro plan starts with a 14-day free trial. You won't be charged until the trial ends.
  </HelpStep>
</HelpSteps>

> A free trial is available once per account. If you've already used yours, upgrading starts your paid Pro plan right away. See [Plans and your free trial](/help/account-billing/plans-and-your-free-trial) for what's included.

## Downgrade your plan

Downgrading is handled in the billing portal.

<HelpSteps>
  <HelpStep title="Open the billing portal">
    Go to **Settings → Subscription & Billing** and click **Manage Payment**. Chiefy opens the secure billing portal in a new tab.
  </HelpStep>
  <HelpStep title="Switch your billing">
    From yearly to monthly, change the plan in the portal. If the portal offers it, you can also move back to Free.
  </HelpStep>
</HelpSteps>

When you downgrade, your current Pro benefits stay active until the end of the billing period you've already paid for. The change takes effect when that period ends — you don't lose anything mid-cycle.

## Cancel your subscription

You can stop a recurring subscription at any time without losing access right away.

<HelpSteps>
  <HelpStep title="Open the billing portal">
    Go to **Settings → Subscription & Billing** and click **Manage Payment** to open the billing portal.
  </HelpStep>
  <HelpStep title="Request cancellation">
    Choose to cancel in the portal and confirm. Your subscription is now set to end — it isn't cut off immediately.
  </HelpStep>
</HelpSteps>

After you cancel, your subscription stays active through the end of the current billing period, so you keep every Pro feature until then. Once that date passes, the status becomes **Canceled** and your account moves to Free.

While a cancellation is scheduled, Chiefy shows an "ending soon" notice on your billing page with the exact end date and a **Keep My Subscription** option — so it's easy to undo if you change your mind.

> Canceling is not the same as deleting your account. Your data and settings stay in place; you simply move from Pro to Free when the period ends.

## Reactivate a canceled subscription

If you scheduled a cancellation but it hasn't taken effect yet, you can turn it back on. Open the billing portal from **Manage Payment** before your period-end date and reactivate, or use the **Keep My Subscription** button on the billing page.

Once the period has fully ended and your subscription shows as **Canceled**, the **Manage Payment** button no longer appears. At that point, returning to Pro means starting a new subscription from the pricing page rather than reactivating the old one.

## Lifetime Pro

Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription — there's nothing to renew, manage, or cancel. Lifetime accounts show a **Lifetime** duration and no **Next payment** date, and the **Manage Payment** button does not appear because there's no recurring billing to manage.
