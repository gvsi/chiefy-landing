Everything about your Pro plan — when it renews, what you paid, and the card behind it — lives in your billing settings and in the secure Stripe portal Chiefy opens for you. This article walks through where to find each of those and what to expect when a payment renews, fails, or is refunded.

## Where billing lives

Open **Settings → Subscription & Billing** in the Chiefy app. There you'll see your current plan, your subscription status, and your **next payment** date. That date is the day your current billing period ends and, for an active subscription, the day the next charge happens.

## When your subscription renews

Monthly and yearly Pro subscriptions **renew automatically** at the end of each billing period. The renewal happens on the date shown as your next payment in **Settings → Subscription & Billing**. Nothing is required from you — the saved payment method is charged for the next period under the same terms.

> Want to stop future renewals? See [Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel](/help/account-billing/upgrade-downgrade-or-cancel). Canceling stops the next charge but keeps your Pro access until the current period ends.

## Your payment method

A valid payment method is **required to start a Pro subscription** — you'll add it at checkout, and it's saved securely by Stripe, our payment processor. Chiefy never stores or sees your full card details.

Tax is calculated automatically at checkout and on each renewal, so the exact amount you're charged depends on your billing address and region.

## View invoices and update your card

To download invoices or change the card on file, open the secure Stripe billing portal from inside Chiefy.

<HelpSteps>
  <HelpStep title="Open billing settings">
    Go to **Settings → Subscription & Billing** in the Chiefy app.
  </HelpStep>
  <HelpStep title="Open the billing portal">
    Choose **Manage Payment**. This opens your secure billing portal hosted by Stripe.
  </HelpStep>
  <HelpStep title="Manage your billing">
    In the portal you can download invoices, update your payment method, and review your full billing history. Changes you make there apply to your subscription right away.
  </HelpStep>
</HelpSteps>

> The Manage Payment button appears for any active recurring subscription — monthly or yearly — including while a payment needs fixing. It's hidden once a subscription is fully canceled, since there's no longer a payment method to manage. Lifetime Pro has no Manage Payment button either (see below).

## If a payment fails

If a renewal charge can't go through, your subscription status changes to **Past Due** or **Unpaid** in **Settings → Subscription & Billing**. To fix it, open **Manage Payment** and update your card or complete the outstanding payment.

Depending on your region and payment method, the charge may be retried automatically before the status changes. If you need help recovering a failed payment, [contact Chiefy support](mailto:hello@chiefy.com).

## Lifetime Pro

Lifetime Pro is a **one-time payment**, not a subscription. There are no recurring charges, no renewals, and no ongoing invoices to manage — once it's purchased, there's nothing to update. You won't see a Manage Payment button, because there's no payment method to maintain.

## Cancellation and refunds

Paid subscription fees are **non-refundable**, except where refunds are required by law. Canceling a subscription stops all future charges, but it does not refund the current billing period or any past payments — you keep Pro access through the end of the period you've already paid for.
