Referral Program Terms

These Referral Program Terms ("Referral Terms") govern Your participation in Chiefy's referral program (the "Referral Program"). By generating or sharing a referral link, or by signing up for Chiefy using someone else's referral link, You agree to these Referral Terms. These Referral Terms supplement, and should be read together with, Our Terms and Conditions. If there is a conflict between these Referral Terms and the Terms and Conditions with respect to the Referral Program, these Referral Terms control.

1. Definitions

  • Referrer means an existing Chiefy user who shares a unique referral link to invite others to use the Service.

  • Referee means a person who signs up for Chiefy using a Referrer's referral link.

  • Reward means the free month of Chiefy Pro described in Section 5 below.

  • First Payment means the Referee's first successful payment for a Chiefy Pro subscription plan. Starting a free trial, by itself, is not a First Payment.

2. Eligibility

To participate as a Referrer, You must have an active Chiefy account and comply with these Referral Terms and Our Terms and Conditions.

To qualify as a Referee under this Referral Program, the referred person must be a genuinely new user of Chiefy who has not previously created a Chiefy account or held a prior trial or subscription. Accounts created to work around this requirement do not qualify, and any resulting Reward may be reversed under Section 7.

3. The Offer

When a Referee completes their First Payment for a Chiefy Pro subscription plan after signing up with a Referrer's referral link, both the Referrer and the Referee earn a Reward: one free month of Chiefy Pro. The Reward is earned when the Referee makes their First Payment, not when the Referee starts a free trial.

4. Eligible Plans

This Referral Program applies only to Chiefy Pro subscription plans. It does not apply to any other Chiefy plan, tier, add-on, or one-time purchase.

5. How the Reward Is Credited

For each Referee who completes a First Payment, the Referrer earns one (1) free month of Chiefy Pro, applied as a credit to the Referrer's active Chiefy Pro subscription.

If the Referrer does not have an active Chiefy Pro subscription at the time a Reward is earned, the Reward is banked and held for up to twelve (12) months from the date it was earned. The Referrer may redeem a banked Reward by activating a Chiefy Pro subscription within that twelve-month period. Reward credits not redeemed within twelve (12) months expire and cannot be reinstated.

6. Prohibited Conduct

You may not:

  • refer Yourself, or use multiple accounts, aliases, or email addresses to generate referrals for Your own benefit;

  • use bots, scripts, or other automated or programmatic means to create referrals or referral traffic; or

  • share Your referral link in a deceptive, misleading, or spammy manner, including through unsolicited bulk email, coupon or deal-listing sites, or paid advertising that misrepresents the Referral Program.

We may disqualify any Referral, Referrer, or Referee that violates this Section, and may suspend or terminate the associated Chiefy account.

7. Reversal of Rewards

We may reverse, revoke, or claw back any Reward, including a Reward already credited or banked, if the Referee's First Payment is refunded, charged back, or disputed, or if We determine, in Our sole discretion, that the Referral was obtained through fraud, abuse, or a violation of these Referral Terms.

8. Non-Transferable; No Cash Value

Reward credits are personal to the Chiefy account that earned them. They are non-transferable and may not be sold, exchanged, gifted, or redeemed for cash, and they have no cash value. Reward credits may only be applied toward that account's own Chiefy Pro subscription.

9. Changes to the Referral Program

We reserve the right, at Our sole discretion, to modify, suspend, or terminate the Referral Program, these Referral Terms, or any Reward, at any time and for any reason, with or without notice. Such changes will not retroactively revoke a Reward already earned and credited or banked in compliance with these Referral Terms as they existed at the time of the qualifying First Payment, except where the Reward is reversed under Section 7.
